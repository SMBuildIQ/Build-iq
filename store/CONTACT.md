## Policy status

Privacy Policy and Terms of Service in the app are **DRAFTS requiring attorney review**.

- Pages are labeled “(Draft)” and show a banner.
- Draft pages send `robots: noindex` until `LEGAL_POLICIES_FINAL=true`.
- Do **not** submit to App Store / Play treating drafts as final counsel-approved policies.
- After attorney approval, set `LEGAL_POLICIES_FINAL=true` and remove draft banners only when counsel signs off on the published text.

## Account deletion correspondence

- In-app: Settings → Account → Delete account  
- Email: support@supplymonkeyco.com (subject: “Account deletion request”)  
- Automated confirmation email (when email delivery is configured) cites Supply Monkey’s name and address in the footer.
